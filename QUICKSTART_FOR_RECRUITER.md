# HireTrack: quick guide (1 page)

**Open the link, sign in with your password.** Works on laptop and phone.

1. **Jobs → New job**: title, city, budget, mandatory and preferred skills. Upload the JD PDF if you have it.
   Add your screening questions under *Screening questions*.
2. **Screening**: pick the job (left sidebar), add a candidate (existing or new), fill the form during the call.
   Press **Save draft** often. Press **Save & mark completed** at the end. Only the name is required.
3. **Compare**: all candidates for the job, ranked. Click a row, then **ℹ️ Why this score?**
   Red ⚠️ means a mandatory skill is missing. Set **your own recommendation** there too.
4. **Data & Backup**: once a week press **Prepare backup file → Download**. Excel sheets for sharing are on the same page.

Tips: the same person can be on several jobs without re-typing. Anything typed under **Other…** is remembered next time.
If the app says it is sleeping, click the wake-up button and wait half a minute. Your data is safe.
